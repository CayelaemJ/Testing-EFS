2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information in connection with the Portal — both information about the platform users who log in (admins, employer contacts) and workforce data supplied by employer clients about their employees for the purpose of financial wellbeing reporting.

3. Personal information we process

Depending on how the Portal is used, we may process:

  • Platform user accounts: name, email address, role, login activity, session data.
  • Workforce/employee data (supplied by employer clients): payroll reference (pseudonymised, not full ID numbers), site/location, income band, employment eligibility dates.
  • Financial wellbeing activity data: debt account status and creditor information, salary advance activity, policy (funeral/credit-life) information, journey/intervention status, referral activity.
  • Chat/support interactions ("Voice of the employee"): anonymised themes, sentiment, and satisfaction data from support conversations.

We do not knowingly collect South African ID numbers, banking account numbers, medical information, or other special personal information as defined by POPIA through the Portal. Employer clients must not upload such information into the Portal.

4. Why we process this information

  • To provide employer clients with reporting and dashboards on the effectiveness of workforce financial wellbeing programmes.
  • To create and manage platform user accounts and control access by role.
  • To communicate with platform users (e.g. account setup, password reset, scheduled reports).
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Portal.
  • To comply with legal and contractual obligations.

5. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal information on the basis of: performance of a contract with the employer client, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Portal, consent (where applicable, e.g. for optional communications), and compliance with legal obligations.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Employer clients, limited to the workforce data relevant to their own employees.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract (e.g. our hosting provider, [e.g. Railway], and email delivery provider), bound by confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Regulators or law enforcement where required by law.

Where any service provider stores or processes data outside South Africa, we take reasonable steps to ensure an adequate level of protection consistent with POPIA's cross-border transfer requirements.

7. Retention

Our default retention profile keeps account, employer, and configuration data for the contract term plus 12 months; reporting history and score snapshots for 25 months; operational, sync, delivery, and audit events for 13 months; and raw imported source files for 90 days after a successful import. A client contract or legal hold may require a longer period. At expiry, data is exported where authorised and securely purged in a scoped, auditable process.

8. Security measures

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure, including encrypted password storage, role-based access control, and access logging. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, and we encourage users to safeguard their own login credentials.

9. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Object to processing of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za) if you believe your information has been processed unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer at the details above. Requests relating to employee workforce data should generally be directed to your employer, who determines how that data is used on the Portal.

10. Cookies

The Portal uses only the cookies necessary for it to function — see our Cookie Notice for details. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.
